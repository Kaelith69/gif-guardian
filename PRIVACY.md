# Gif Guardian Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Overview

Gif Guardian is a Reddit moderation tool designed to help subreddit moderators identify specified GIPHY GIFs and manage associated AutoModerator rules.

This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be processed in connection with the tool, how that information may be used, and the factors that determine its storage, retention, and disclosure.

## 2. Information Collection

Gif Guardian does not intentionally collect personal information solely because someone views or uses its publicly available moderation rules.

Depending on the implementation, deployment, and enabled features, the following information may be processed:

* **Reddit account information:** Account identifiers or other information necessary for authorized moderation operations.
* **Subreddit configuration:** Subreddit settings, AutoModerator rules, and related configuration data.
* **GIF identifiers:** GIPHY GIF identifiers and associated matching rules used to identify specified content.
* **Moderation events:** Information about matching content and moderation actions, where supported by the implementation.
* **Technical information:** Error reports, diagnostic information, and technical logs generated by the deployed system.

The information actually processed depends on the features enabled, the permissions granted, and the services integrated with the deployment. No information collection practice should be assumed unless it is supported by the actual implementation.

## 3. Use of Information

Information processed by Gif Guardian may be used only as reasonably necessary to:

* Identify content matching configured GIF identifiers.
* Create, maintain, or update associated AutoModerator rules.
* Support authorized subreddit moderation operations.
* Diagnose technical issues and investigate errors.
* Maintain the reliability, security, and functionality of the tool.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes unless otherwise disclosed and permitted by applicable law and platform policies.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Data storage and sharing depend on the deployed implementation and its integrations.

Configuration data, identifiers, moderation records, and technical logs may be stored when required by the tool's functionality. The storage location, access controls, and retention periods depend on the services and infrastructure used.

Information may be accessible to authorized maintainers or service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, or troubleshoot the tool.

The project does not guarantee that information is never stored, transmitted, or shared. Such claims require verification against the actual implementation and deployment.

## 5. Third Party Services

Gif Guardian may interact with Reddit, GIPHY related services, and other third party services required by its implementation.

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, terms, and technical practices. Gif Guardian does not control the independent data processing activities of third party providers.

Users and subreddit moderators should review the applicable third party policies before authorizing integrations or granting permissions.

## 6. Security and Data Retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be applied to information processed or stored by the deployed system. These safeguards may include access controls, secure credential handling, and appropriate protection of configuration files and technical logs.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable technical requirements, operational needs, and legal obligations. Actual retention periods depend on the implementation and configuration.

## 7. Your Choices and Permissions

Reddit users and subreddit moderators should review the permissions granted to Gif Guardian and any connected services.

Where applicable, users and moderators may revoke permissions, disconnect integrations, or request the removal of information under the control of the project maintainer, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal obligations.

Revoking access may affect the tool's functionality. Information independently retained by Reddit or other third party services remains subject to their respective policies and procedures.

## 8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Gif Guardian's functionality, integrations, deployment, or data practices change.

Revised versions will be published in the project's public GitHub repository. The **Last updated** date will be changed to reflect the latest revision.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, concerns, or requests relating to Gif Guardian, contact the project maintainer through the project's public GitHub repository.
